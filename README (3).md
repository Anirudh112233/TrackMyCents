# **TrackMyCents** 💰

**Take control of your finances with TrackMyCents!**  
An all-in-one expense management solution that simplifies budgeting, tracks spending, and helps you achieve your financial goals effortlessly.  

---

## 🚀 **Key Features**

### 📊 **Expense Tracking**
- Effortlessly record daily expenses with categorized inputs.  
- Visualize spending patterns with **detailed reports and interactive charts**.  
- Edit or delete transactions for complete control.  

### 💡 **Budget Manager**
- Set **customizable monthly or weekly budget goals**.  
- Receive timely alerts when nearing your budget limit.  
- Track and improve your budgeting habits over time.  

### 💵 **Money Saver**
- Spot and eliminate unnecessary expenses with **smart suggestions**.  
- Set and monitor personalized **savings goals**.  
- Access actionable tips for everyday savings.  

### 📑 **Report Generation**
- Create professional-grade spending reports in **PDF or Excel formats**.  
- Perfect for record-keeping and sharing your financial summaries.
